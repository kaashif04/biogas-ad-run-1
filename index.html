Key findings

  • Methane produced most of the run: ≈ 80–1,600 ppm in the chamber vs 15–20 ppm in clean air.
  • Peak: 17–28 Sep, both methane sensors high (≈ 120–320 ppm).
  • Upset and recovery: hydrogen spike 8 Sep 16:00 – 10 Sep 08:00 (≈ 2 → 170 ppm), weak methane 11–15 Sep, full recovery 17 Sep.
  • End of digestion: CO₂ 2,820 → 1,185 ppm (1–6 Oct); methane down to ≈ 50–90 ppm.
  • Temperature: warmer days gave more methane (r = 0.59 from 17 Sep). Coolest days, 21–23 Sep, match the CO₂ dip.
  • Check with lab notes: 8 Sep (feeding?) and 1–2 Sep (CO₂ with no methane).
